Welcome to Skanyxx

Skanyxx is your company's agent workspace. This installs the desktop launcher: skanyxx opens your company's Skanyxx in the browser. The Skanyxx server itself is installed with the Helm chart, not here.

This installer will place Skanyxx in /usr/local/share/skanyxx and add a command-line launcher at /usr/local/bin/skanyxx.

After installation, set your company's Skanyxx address as Skanyxx:RemoteUrl in /usr/local/share/skanyxx/appsettings.json.
